# GrowWise Open World

GrowWise now starts in an outdoor exploration world. The detailed farming grid remains available through the GrowWise farm gate.

## Controls

- `WASD` / Arrow keys — walk
- `Shift` — sprint while travel energy remains
- `E` — interact with a location, NPC, or resource
- `Tab` — open/close the world map; while inside the detailed farm, return to the open world
- `T` — cycle travel mode: walk, bicycle, utility cart
- `I` — inventory
- `M` — machinery center
- `F4` — auto-farm mode

## Regions

1. Greenfield Farm — farm gate, animal barn, machinery yard
2. Blue Riverbank — dock and fishing
3. Seedling Village — shop, market, laboratory, processing hub
4. Community Forest — forage resources and survey sites
5. Highland Source — mine, stone, minerals, and headwater survey
6. Retention Wetland — water management, wetland resources, and wildlife

## World systems

- Continuous outdoor traversal with camera follow
- Region and point-of-interest discovery
- Daily resource gathering and respawn
- Moving NPC routes and daily conversations
- Dynamic weather, time-of-day lighting, and world events
- World quests and rewards
- Minimap and fast travel to discovered hubs
- Sprint stamina and unlockable travel vehicles
- Backward-compatible save/load for open-world state

## Farm transition

Walk to the GrowWise farm gate and press `E` to enter the detailed farming view. Press `Tab` inside the farm to return to the outdoor world. All crop, water, machinery, livestock, laboratory, processing, town, and economy systems remain available.
